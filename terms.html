Guest Guidelines & Policies

Terms of Dining

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Wok & Raga Restro: Our guidelines are designed to ensure an exceptional, respectful, and safe dining experience for every guest in Diphu.

1. Table Reservations & Confirmations

Reservation requests sent through our website are submitted via WhatsApp and manually confirmed by our maître d'. Reservations are held for up to 15 minutes past the scheduled time before being released during peak dining hours.

2. Dine-In & Food Preparation

All our signature wok dishes and handmade momos are prepared fresh to order in live high-heat woks. During peak hours, please allow adequate preparation time for artisan dishes.

3. Special Dietary & Allergy Inquiries

Guests with specific food allergies or dietary preferences (e.g. Jain, vegetarian, spice tolerance) are warmly invited to notify our service staff when placing their order.

4. Private Events & Photography

Guests are welcome to take personal photographs at our signature Sanctuary Pod and rattan seating areas. For private event bookings or acoustic sessions, please contact our desk in advance.
