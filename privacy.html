Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Notice: This Privacy Policy outlines how Wok & Raga Restro (Diphu, Karbi Anglong, Assam) handles customer inquiries, reservations, and communications.

1. Direct Communication & Reservations

When you initiate a table reservation or food order through our website, your request is generated client-side and routed directly to Wok & Raga via WhatsApp or direct telephone call. We do not store, sell, or monetize your personal conversation details on third-party marketing databases.

2. Information Collected

Information provided voluntarily by customers (such as name, guest count, reservation time, and dietary requests) is used exclusively by our restaurant staff to fulfill dining reservations, take-out orders, and customer service inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes trusted external services for essential functionality:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): Used for secure, direct communication and booking confirmations.
  • Google Maps: Used to provide driving directions and geographic location assistance to our restaurant in Diphu, Assam.
  • Google Fonts: Used to render typography.
