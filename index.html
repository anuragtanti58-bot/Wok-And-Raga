Where Flavour Finds Rhythm.

Wok & Raga brings together the soulful richness of Indian spices and the intense heat of wok-tossed Chinese cuisine. Born from a profound love for hospitality, music, and artful dining, we welcome you to Diphu’s premier dining destination.

Every dish is an acoustic dialogue: the sizzle of wok hei against cast iron, aromatic garlic simmering in regional Assam botanicals, and hand-ground spice blends made fresh each dawn.

Heritage & Passion

More Than a Meal — An Ode to Fire & Melody.

In classical Indian music, a Raga is an expressive framework that colors the mind and evokes deep emotional resonance. The Wok is the fiery vessel where heat, steam, and raw ingredients are transformed in seconds.

At Wok & Raga Restro, we fused both worlds. Located peacefully in Diphu near Aturkimi Academy, we set out to build a space where food lovers wouldn’t have to compromise between vibrant flavors and an inspiring, comfortable aesthetic.
